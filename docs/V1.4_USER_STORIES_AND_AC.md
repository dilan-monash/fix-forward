# v1.4 prototype user stories and acceptance criteria

These are **candidate** stories for BA/mentor review. They do not replace the approved LeanKit baseline until accepted.

## US-G01 — Start with my goal

**As a household user with a faulty appliance, I want to start with what I am trying to do so that I do not have to understand FixForward's internal process.**

Acceptance:
- Home shows Repair, Compare, Recycle and I'm not sure.
- Each route still passes through the quick safety/recall gate.
- A serious warning or possible recall overrides the user's chosen shortcut.
- After a clear gate, Repair/Compare/Recycle go directly to the requested tool.

## US-G02 — Understand safety in ordinary language

**As a user who may not know electrical or recall terminology, I want simple explanations so that I can answer safely.**

Acceptance:
- “Product recall” is explained on the home/recall experience.
- Questions do not require the user to understand “arcing”, “provenance” or similar technical terms.
- Questions change according to appliance type where supported.
- No result says the appliance is guaranteed safe.

## US-G03 — Find useful nearby service information

**As a Melbourne user, I want practical service information and a map so that I can take action without reading a dataset.**

Acceptance:
- Optional current-location button asks browser permission only after click.
- Denial falls back to manual suburb/postcode.
- Exact coordinates are not sent to FixForward/Neon.
- Nearby results use available service coordinates and straight-line distance.
- Primary cards show practical fields first.
- Missing phone/hours/acceptance are not invented.
- Technical provenance remains available in expandable details.

## US-G04 — Trust cost information

**As a user comparing repair and replacement, I want prices to come from real evidence so that the app does not look precise when it is guessing.**

Acceptance:
- No automatic price is shown when governed evidence is insufficient.
- Product matching and price evidence are separate stages.
- Future fuzzy/AI matching can suggest an identity but cannot create a dollar value.
- Evidence specificity is labelled exact model / brand / broad category.
- Manual comparison remains neutral and validates inputs.
