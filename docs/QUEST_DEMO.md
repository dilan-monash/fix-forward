# FixForward Quest: mentor demonstration

Quest is an adventure for children aged 7–12. Demonstrate a child choosing, discovering and creating; a companion is optional. This walkthrough describes current implemented controls. It is not a completed child study, an educational-effectiveness claim or the final verification record for the creative refresh. Allow about 10–15 minutes and follow the audience's pace. The game is untimed.

Start the local server using [QUEST_IMPLEMENTATION.md](QUEST_IMPLEMENTATION.md), then keep these two tabs open:

- Quest: http://127.0.0.1:5502/quest
- Existing adult FixForward: http://127.0.0.1:5502/

The adult preview uses explicitly labelled **SYNTHETIC USABILITY TEST DATA**. Its example records are not live recall or service information. Use only clearly identified demonstration inputs. Child missions use their own authored fictional stories. Nothing is published by this walkthrough.

Use one browser/profile and origin for the save-and-return steps. To show a fresh first visit, use a separate profile or clear only your own demonstration progress with **Settings → Reset my adventure** and its confirmation.

## 1. Meet a guide and solve a story

1. Show Home Corner, Second-Chance Studio and Collection Station. Tap **Pip** or **Flo** beside the map for a short reply. The invitation is “Help Pip and Flo find a next chapter.” The boundary is “Your adventure stays on screen. Real appliances need adult help.”
2. Choose **Guided**, then **Start a mission**. A returning player may see **Continue your adventure** or **Choose a different mission**. Select **Flo**, then **Flo's next home**.
3. Notice Flo's dialogue. Choose **Look for clues**. Open **Sam's check**, then **Keep exploring**. Do the same with **Bea's invitation**. Both are now in **Your clue pocket**; reopen one from the pocket to show that clues remain available.
4. Choose **Make a plan**. For the deliberate mentor-demo mistake, tap **Choose disposal straight away**, then the **What happens next?** space. Choose **See what happens**.
5. Read Flo's reaction and the clue-based explanation. Choose **Try my plan again**, then **Sam arranges Flo's new home**, and place it in the same space. Choose **See what happens**, then **See the next chapter**.
6. Show the new scene and Flo's closing line. Reuse follows the supplied completed checks and interested household; no real fan is being certified.

Use Tab/Shift+Tab and Enter or Space on the same controls to show the keyboard path. Dragging is optional; tapping an action and its space makes the same decision.

## 2. Look back, collect a stamp and make something

1. At the outcome, use **Your story projector**: select **Before your choice**, then **The next chapter**. Show how the same scene changes. This switch adds no extra progress.
2. Point to the earned story stamp, then choose **Create my story postcard**.
3. In **Set the mood**, choose **starlight** or **mint**. In **Leave your mark**, choose **leaf** or **spark**. The preview changes immediately. There is no name field or forced sharing.
4. Choose **Save my postcard** to request an SVG download to this device. Inspect the actual downloaded file before claiming the export succeeded. The browser controls its download location; this story's filename is `fixforward-quest-flo-next-home.svg`.
5. Choose **Back to my Discovery Book**. Find the earned stamp in **Your story passport**, open it and confirm that the selected design returns. Reload and recheck when demonstrating persistence.
6. In the book, choose an earned decoration, then a location such as **Home Corner**. A drag from the decoration's handle is another option. The eight stamps represent eight stories; sorting discoveries and decorations do not silently complete missions.

Saving a design in browser progress and downloading its picture are different actions. Neither sends a postcard to another person or uploads it to a service.

## 3. Change the evidence while keeping the fan

Choose **Choose another mission** from an outcome, or open an unearned stamp from the map/book. Select **The quiet fan**. Open **A quiet fan** and **Empty report**.

Use **Ask Sam to arrange a repairer's check**. The outcome contacts Jo, a qualified repairer; it does not guess a broken part or promise a repair. Invite the mentor to identify what changed between this fan and Flo's previously checked reuse story.

## 4. Play five pictures at Collection Station

1. Return to **Mission map** or **Neighbourhood**, open **Collection Station**, then **Play sorting**. An unfinished round resumes; **Continue sorting** is also available from the map.
2. Read each card's condition. The opening says story appliances have been checked for collection, and that a warning or missing clue changes the plan.
3. Drag one picture by **Pick up** into a labelled destination. Answer another by tapping its destination or using keyboard activation.
4. Optionally release outside all destinations to show that it records no answer. A wrong destination reveals a clue and **Try another place**. **Show me a clue** is available before answering.
5. Use **Next picture** between answers and **Finish this round** for the fifth. Show the separate independent/helped counts, then offer the choice to explore the book, play again or **Finish for now**.

The current labels are **E-waste collection**, **Paper & cardboard recycling**, and **Pause & ask**. The first means Sam must confirm an appropriate service accepts the item. Paper answers use the stated Merri-bek context. “Pause & ask” leads to trusted-adult help for warnings or missing information; it does not teach children to handle the device.

On a phone-sized viewport, demonstrate the object and touch-sized controls. Say whether input came from a mouse/desktop pointer or an actual touch device. Resizing a desktop browser is not real-device touch evidence.

## 5. Show optional support without making it the main journey

Open **The kettle's second chance**. Select **Secret envelope** or **Open the secret envelope** to read **Jo's report**. It can be revealed alone or with a companion, then closed with **Back to my mission**. The child can continue without someone else operating the controls.

In **Settings**, choose **Less movement**. Revisit a story outcome/projector and check that the same information is visible without greeting, reveal or stamp motion. Normal-mode story effects run once and last no more than 580 ms. Try **Read aloud** if supported; browser voices vary and text remains available.

## 6. Open the separate adult baseline

Use footer **About this adventure**, not a header adult-mode switch. Show sources, the fictional-story boundary, local sorting context and privacy explanation. Select **Open adult FixForward** to open a separate `/` tab. No fictional condition, repair report or recall answer transfers to it.

Return to the original adult tab without reloading to inspect any earlier demonstration answers. Adult state is in memory; refreshing that tab is a separate action. The adult baseline is a comparison reference, not the proposed future adult redesign.

End by distinguishing the questions: can a child choose, understand a changed clue and make a creation they want to keep? Can an adult identify a practical next step and understand uncertainty? Use [QUEST_COMPARISON_WORKSHEET.md](QUEST_COMPARISON_WORKSHEET.md) for later approved observations. Continuing, downloading and playing together are all optional.
