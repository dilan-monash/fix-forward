# v1.4 usability test — neutral task script

## Purpose

Test whether a normal metropolitan Melbourne household user can complete useful tasks without understanding the project, datasets or technical terminology.

Do not explain the interface unless the participant is completely stuck. Record what they do and say.

## Scenario 1 — repair goal

> Your vacuum cleaner has stopped working. You would prefer to repair it if possible. Use FixForward to work out what you would do next.

Observe:
- do they choose **I want to repair it** immediately?
- do they understand why the quick safety questions appear?
- do they understand the recall explanation?
- can they use current location or manual suburb?
- can they find address / call / directions without opening source details?

## Scenario 2 — safety override

> Imagine the appliance has a burning smell. Continue using the site as you normally would.

Pass condition: participant understands they should stop using it for now and is not offered a community Repair Café as the primary next step.

## Scenario 3 — compare costs

> You want to know whether repair is worth comparing with replacement. Start from the home page.

Observe whether the participant understands that automatic pricing is not yet available and whether the manual fallback is understandable without thinking it is financial advice.

## Scenario 4 — recycle

> You have decided you do not want to keep the appliance. Find somewhere appropriate to take it.

Observe location/map/filter usability and whether the user notices the “check before travelling” instruction.

## Scenario 5 — no location permission

> When the browser asks for your location, choose Don't allow.

Pass condition: user can continue with suburb/postcode and does not feel blocked.

## Post-task questions

Ask in the participant's own words:

1. What does FixForward help you do?
2. What do you think a product recall is?
3. Why did the site ask safety questions?
4. Was anything unnecessary or confusing?
5. If you wanted a repair place, what information did you actually care about?
6. Did you understand what happened to your location information?
7. Which part would you remove if you could?

## Metrics

Record:
- completion / failed / assisted;
- time to first meaningful action;
- wrong turns/backtracks;
- terms they asked about;
- whether they opened source/provenance details voluntarily;
- whether they could contact/navigate to a service;
- confidence score 1–5 after each task.
