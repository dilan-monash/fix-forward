# Retail price observations

Reviewed on 11 September 2026 for the FixForward prototype.

This seed contains 18 factual Australian advertised-price observations across 10 appliance categories. Each included amount was visible on the retailer's product page and agreed with the corresponding category listing. These are a small curated sample, not a complete market survey, a price history, a live feed, or evidence of a product's reliability.

## How to read the records

- `observedAt` is the date the source was reviewed for this catalogue, not a retailer price-change date.
- Web retrieval sometimes returned a cached product-page snapshot. The crawl age reported by the retrieval tool is recorded below and in the affected record's notes. No claim is made that a purchase at this price is currently available.
- The amount is the full advertised purchase price in AUD, before delivery and optional extras. Instalment amounts, conditional price-beat offers, StoreCash, ticket prices, and assumed discounts are excluded.
- `availability: "not-verified"` is intentional. An Add to Cart button alone does not verify postcode-specific stock.
- A category match is an example, not an equivalent replacement. Compare capacity, form factor, included accessories, and features.
- These prices provide cost context only. Do not infer fault diagnosis, repairability, service life, electrical safety, or a brand's reliability from them.
